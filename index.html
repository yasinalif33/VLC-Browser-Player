    What this player does

    Everything VLC is known for that a browser can honestly do — no installs, no uploads, no server in between.

    • Local files
      MP4, WebM, MKV*, MP3, FLAC, WAV, OGG and more — whatever your browser can decode, it plays.
    • Network streams
      Paste a direct media URL and it joins the playlist like any file.
    • Subtitles
      Drop an .srt or .vtt file onto the playing video — it's converted and shown instantly.
    • Playlist & queue
      Reorder by playing, loop one / loop all, shuffle, previous & next.
    • Effects
      Live brightness, contrast, saturation and hue — VLC's classic adjustments, in the browser.
    • Snapshots & PiP
      Save the current frame as a PNG, or pop the video out into picture-in-picture.

    * MKV and some codecs depend on your browser's decoder support. If a file won't play here, the desktop VLC will still play it — this page never alters your files.
